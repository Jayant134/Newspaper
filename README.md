# Newspaper

A news app that lets users to scroll through news according to categories and save favourite news. To read a news in detail, just click on it.

## Download APK

⬇️ Download the latest APK from the Releases section:

https://github.com/Jayant134/Newspaper/releases/download/v1.0/Newspaper.apk

## How to Run

1. Download the APK from the link above.
2. Install it on your Android device.
3. Open the app and explore general news on home screen.
4. For reading category wise news, click on menu on top right corner and select a category.
5. To read a news in detail, click on it.
6. Swipe right to save a news.
7. In saved news, swipe left to delete it.
